How to Play — Ember Court

A 40-card deck: four suits — Ember 🔥, Tide 💧, Gale 🍃, Stone 🪨 — ranks 1–10. A match lasts 7 rounds (tricks). You duel the Cinder Queen.

  • The leader plays any card. The follower must follow the led suit if able.
  • Highest card of the led suit wins the trick — except Ember is trump: any Ember beats any non-Ember card.
  • Exception: Tide douses Ember — a Tide card beats an Ember card when the two meet.
  • The trick winner scores embers equal to the sum of both cards' ranks, and leads the next round.
  • Kindle: win 3 tricks in a row with the same suit and bank +5 bonus embers.
  • First to 21 embers, or most embers after 7 rounds, wins the match.

Read the Queen. After every round a Cinder Tell reveals one of her habits — she leads high Tide early, hoards Ember for the late rounds, and dumps low Stone when a trick is lost. Learn it, and take her flame.
